Information Collected

We do not sell your data, and this codebase does not include any advertising or third-party analytics/tracking. That said, you should check to see who is hosting this site, as it may not be the original creators; the original creators have no control over what is added to each individual self-hosted instance.

Account information

If you register an account, we store your username and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store or can see your actual password). Providing an email address is optional and is used only for password recovery - it's never shown to other players.

Gameplay data

We track statistics about games you play (wins, losses, in-game actions, Elo rating, achievements) and a history of your recent matches, in order to power profiles and leaderboards. You can control what's visible to other players via your Profile Privacy settings.

Chat messages

Messages you send in lobby or in-game chat are visible to other players present in that lobby or game, consistent with the room's chat settings.

Technical information

We log IP addresses for the purposes of rate limiting and enforcing bans/timeouts against abusive accounts. Your browser's local storage is used to keep you logged in and remember preferences like your chosen card deck, sound pack, and avatar - this data stays in your browser and isn't sent anywhere else.

How Information Is Used

  • To create and maintain your account and let you log in
  • To track stats, achievements, and leaderboard rankings
  • To operate chat features during games and in the lobby
  • To detect and act on abusive behavior (bans, timeouts, rate limiting)
  • To send password reset emails, if you've provided an email address and requested one

Who Can See Your Information

Your username, join date, games played, and Elo rating are visible to other players by default. Win rate, individual stats, achievements, and match history can each be individually hidden via your account's Privacy settings. Your email address, if provided, is never shown to other users.

If this site's operator has configured outgoing email (for password resets), your email address is shared with [EMAIL PROVIDER, e.g. Gmail / Resend / SendGrid - or "N/A" if not configured] solely to deliver that email.

Data Retention

Account and gameplay data is retained for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account at any time from the Settings page. Please note that match history you share with others may still show your old username in their historical records, as that history is not tied to your account.

If you are unable to access your account for some reason and wish for it to be deleted, please contact a site administrator.
